Focus, the app I'm building

A planner that turns what I say into jobs, plans the day around Sav, and runs on my phone and a kitchen tablet. These are mockups, not the real app yet. This version adds 20 new screens at the end, marked New.

  • 1Swipe left and right to move through the screens.
  • 2Scroll down on phone screens, some are long.
  • 3Tap Tablet, then turn your phone sideways.
  • 4Try Light and Dark. Tell me which you prefer and what's confusing.

Turn your phone sideways

The tablet screens are landscape, like the tablet on the kitchen wall. If your screen doesn't turn, check rotation lock is off.
